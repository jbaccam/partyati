# Creator Store assets

These free models were imported through Roblox Studio and converted to static prop geometry. Only visible Parts and their SpecialMeshes are retained. Gameplay code, scripts, sounds, animations, welds, and vehicle behavior are not included. Local data lives in `src/shared/AssetData.luau`; original creators retain ownership of their work.

| Prop | Creator | Original asset | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skateboard | Roblox | [Fancy Skateboard](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/101851234) | Static display prop with a normalized footprint |
| Tent | Meleniam | [Simple Camping Tent](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/341666811) | Static camping prop, normalized footprint |
| Pine scenery | StoneSymmetry | [Big Realistic Pine Tree](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/73454171742334) | Two textured meshes, normalized and instanced along the shoreline |
| Cliff scenery | bearduckmonkey | [Rock Cliff](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/4113251429) | Textured rock mesh, repeated at varied scales to form the cove and distant cliffs |

Other game props and stage geometry are generated by this project's code.
