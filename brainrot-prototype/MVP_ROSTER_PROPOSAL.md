# Five-fighter MVP proposal

Tung Sahur is implemented. The four additions below are recommendations, not approved scope or implemented characters. Their numbers are fast-paced starting points for playtesting, not measured balance. Other fighters' existing twelve-character design definitions have not been changed.

| Fighter | Role | HP | Walk / sprint | Main weakness |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Tung Sahur | Close-range brawler | 115 | 20 / 28 (implemented) | Limited ranged pressure |
| Tralalero Tralala | Aerial rushdown | 100 | 22 / 30 | Fragile when a committed dash misses |
| Chimpanzini Bananini | Mobile trickster | 105 | 21 / 29 | Needs setups; less direct burst |
| Ballerina Cappuccina | Precision duelist | 95 | 23 / 31 | Short reach; punishable failed counter |
| Bombardiro Crocodilo | Mobile explosives fighter | 120 | 19 / 27 | Large hurtbox and recovery after attacks |

## Proposed kits

- Tralalero: M1 fin/jaw combo; Q short shark torpedo; E tail-sweep launcher; R wave-breach recovery; F three steerable, clearly warned shark rushes. No permanent flight or free repeated dashes. Good first follow-up to Tung for testing melee matchups.
- Chimpanzini: M1 ape punches; Q outbound/return banana boomerang; E one visible peel that pops rather than long-stuns; R banana vault for recovery; F banana barrage with a heavy final fruit impact. Limit active projectiles/traps and reward prediction over spam.
- Ballerina: M1 short dance-kick combo; Q traveling pirouette; E brief counter with meaningful miss recovery; R grand-jete leap; F a telegraphed traveling dance combination and launching finish. Fastest and hardest to master, not strongest by default.
- Bombardiro: M1 wing/snout attacks; Q one aimed bomb; E short forward bombing run; R afterburner recovery; F a warned three-pass airstrike. Keep him grounded outside limited movement abilities. Avoid infinite hovering, offscreen unanswerable bombardment, and instant area-wide damage.

## Balance rules

### Melee versus ranged — proposed, not implemented

Tralalero should remain primarily a melee rushdown character. A shark dash, fin/jaw combo, and tail launcher do not make him a long-range counterpick to Tung. Bombardiro can be the projectile specialist, but every fighter should have a useful close-range basic attack.

Prevent endless kiting structurally: no full-speed sprint while firing, a brief visible firing/recovery commitment, finite projectile range, limited attack frequency, and no permanent airborne retreat. Keep projectile travel dodgeable and avoid repeated long hit-stuns from distant poke. Melee should have better sustained damage after closing distance, not merely higher HP while absorbing unavoidable shots. Give Tung a real steerable approach option as a separate future change (for example forward travel on Dawn Call), with a cooldown and punishable miss.

Arena geometry matters: several approach routes, cover that actually blocks projectiles, and no inaccessible camping platforms or endless unobstructed retreat lanes. Test Tung versus the first projectile prototype at equal player skill before committing the other full kits. A winning melee approach must offer a real punish window; a well-spaced ranged fighter must still have ways to defend. Do not guarantee either side a win or tune from raw unadjusted match win rates.

Equal overall power, different strengths. Later unlocks can have higher mastery ceilings, not blanket stat upgrades. Every launch needs an escape opportunity; every powerful attack needs a visible windup or punishable recovery. Reward chase and approach with movement tools rather than giving everyone projectiles. Preserve directional air control. Use readable terrain and ledges at the new movement speeds.

Start with the same basic jump/air-jump rules and the same Core requirement for every fighter. Differentiate recovery trajectories before adding extra jumps or charges. Keep basic attack startup responsive and avoid long full-control locks. Test skill-matched 1v1s for matchup balance and free-for-all rounds separately for area-damage snowballing, survivability, and third-party kills. Track win rate, damage, recovery success, missed-attack punishment, and time spent unable to act; a raw aggregate win-rate number is not enough.

Recommended build order: Tralalero, Chimpanzini, Ballerina, Bombardiro (whose unusual rig and flight-like recovery are higher implementation risks).
