# Reference skin analysis and revision 2

Observed directly in the six user-supplied images, especially the close rear view, side view, and frontal face close-up:

## Surface

- The base is uneven ivory/beige, with desaturated gray areas. It is not a flat white material.
- Fine pores and speckles sit on top of broader mottling. Fine wrinkles have direction: stretched down limbs and compressed across bent joints.
- Faint branching blue-gray veins sit beneath the pale skin. Their visibility varies across a body region.
- Pink, rusty red, and purple-brown discoloration concentrates around ears, palms, wrists, elbows, knees, ankles, and the edges of hanging skin. These patches have broken, diffuse edges rather than solid red bands.
- The surface reads as dry and aged; occasional smoother areas catch light differently. Uniform roughness and uniform noise erase this variation.

## Anatomy and sag

- Bony landmarks remain visible through loose skin: collarbones, shoulder blades, elbows, knees, shin tendons, ankles, and Achilles tendons.
- The chest has broad hanging sheets of skin, with stretched attachment points and heavier lower edges. The reference does not have six identical exposed horizontal ribs.
- The lower abdomen transitions into several overlapping, uneven folds hanging well over the groin. The layers differ in width, depth, sag, and center position.
- Jaw folds extend below the chin beside the neck. Elbow folds form loose pockets whose sag depends on the arm bend.
- The foot has a posterior heel pad, a narrower Achilles tendon above it, distinct ankle bones, a raised instep, and a wider forefoot. The earlier oval foot did not establish these landmarks.

## Implemented changes

- Replaced repeated chest/abdomen tubes with individually shaped closed skin sheets, fused into the body.
- Added asymmetric hanging chest, jaw, elbow, and pelvic shapes, including a longer layered groin apron.
- Added clavicles, more tapered thigh/calf forms, heel pads, Achilles tendons, ankle bones, instep tendons, and varied toe sizes.
- Added non-repeating sculpt relief, spatially varied joint redness, fold-edge coloration, pallor, speckles, subtle vein patterns, and fine directional wrinkle/porosity bump.
- Baked 4096-pixel color, tangent-space normal, and roughness maps. Preserved the higher-resolution sculpture as an editable hidden source in Blender.

This is procedural modeling guided by reference observations, not a scan of the source character. The practical test is the updated front, side, back, and detail renders; Roblox lighting will also affect its final appearance.
