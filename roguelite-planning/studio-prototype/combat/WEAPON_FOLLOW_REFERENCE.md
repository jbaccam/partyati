## Independent attack timing replaces the ordered cadence

The user found the evenly spaced attacks too mechanical. Removed slot ordering, timing ranks and evenly spaced phases. Each slot now has an independent server-owned random generator. With multiple targetable weapons, first/reacquired attacks receive independent offsets within 0–80% of their own cooldown; intervals are not reserved, and several weapons may start in the same heartbeat. A weapon never waits for another weapon to attack or finish.

With multiple weapons equipped, each subsequent interval varies independently from 90–110% of its resolved cooldown, retaining the nominal mean interval before heartbeat quantization. This lets relative attack order change and attacks overlap or cluster instead of repeating a fixed sequence. Intervals cannot be shorter than the weapon's animation duration. Single-equipped-weapon cadence is unchanged; a lone targetable weapon gets no opening delay. Excalibur reach/return, damage and other weapon behavior remain unchanged.

## Changes

The previous server recomputed the radius at 30 Hz using every body's animated position and half the largest weapon's full 3D bounding diagonal, plus a margin. This both produced arm-driven pulsing and made an upright sword's length increase horizontal spacing.

`WeaponMotion.radius` now uses only HumanoidRootPart width, with a 2.6-stud standard radius and bounded avatar scaling. Neither limbs, accessories, equipped weapon length nor animation poses affect it. This same replicated radius drives server attack origins and client anchors. Weapon size and the six 60-degree slot assignments are preserved.

Client visuals have a separate position spring per slot. The goal is the root-relative slot; the spring follows its world-space travel and can cross the body during turns. An analytic underdamped solver tracks a linearly moving goal (frequency parameter 15 radians/second, damping ratio 0.68). Idle facing interpolates toward character heading at an exponential rate of 14/second. Explicit target aim and attack rotation retain the shared combat pose. Existing vertical idle/run bob is retained; the additional synthetic sideways sway is removed.

Jump lag is limited to 3.5 studs vertically. Horizontal goal separation allows a full slot diameter, so an instant 180-degree turn does not trigger a corrective snap. Teleports over 32 studs and suspended frames over 0.25 seconds reset follow state. Respawning, replacing a weapon, or hiding it also resets the relevant state. Attack float strength eases toward 0.45 instead of changing instantly. Rendered parts are explicitly anchored, noncolliding, nontouching and excluded from spatial queries.

Only visual following is delayed. Server target selection, cooldowns, damage, obstruction checks and hit poses remain authoritative. Closer slot spacing changes the locations from which existing attacks are evaluated; combat coverage needs live validation.
